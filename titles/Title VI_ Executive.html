Subchapter I: Harrison County Department of Parks and Recreation; Director
10
6 H.C.S. § 1101There is an executive department of Harrison County, the Harrison County Department of Parks and Recreation (“Department” or “HCPR”), responsible for the administration, operation, maintenance, and stewardship of County parks, trails, recreational facilities, waterways, nature reserves, natural areas, and other public lands or facilities placed under its care by Resolution of the Council (collectively, “County lands and facilities administered by HCPR”).ACTIVE
11
6 H.C.S. § 1101(a)⤷HCPR is further designated as the County department with countywide responsibility for the regulation, administration, and stewardship of fishing, hunting, wildlife, conservation, and other natural resource-related activities, regardless of land ownership, as authorized by County law.ACTIVE
12
6 H.C.S. § 1102There is a Director of Parks and Recreation (“Director”) who shall be appointed by the County Executive, confirmed by the Council, and shall exercise such authority as may be necessary to carry out the Director’s duties under this Act and other County law.
ACTIVE
13
6 H.C.S. § 1103The Department may establish and remove such divisions, programs, and services as are necessary to carry out its responsibilities, including conservation, outdoor recreation, public education, facility operations, and emergency readiness.ACTIVE

Subchapter II: Powers; Park Administration; Conservation
15
6 H.C.S. § 1201The Department shall manage County lands and facilities administered by HCPR by establishing and administering rules and permit systems governing access, hours, camping, reservations, facility use, recreational activities, environmental protection, visitor conduct, and the lawful use, taking, and conservation of fish, wildlife, and other natural resources, where permitted by law.ACTIVE
16
6 H.C.S. § 1202The Department shall provide and coordinate public safety and protective services within County lands and facilities administered by HCPR, including patrol, first aid, water safety and rescue, search and rescue, emergency response, and the enforcement of applicable County laws, rules, and regulations.ACTIVE

Subchapter III: Park Rangers; Authority and Duties
18

6 H.C.S. § 1302Park Rangers are not peace officers by virtue of this Act and shall not make arrests; however, when assigned or acting in an enforcement, investigative, protective, or public safety capacity, and in addition to any other duties assigned under this Act, Park Rangers are authorized, within the scope of their official duties, to exercise the following powers and responsibilities:ACTIVE
20
6 H.C.S. § 1302(a)⤷Provide public safety and protective services within County lands and facilities administered by HCPR, including patrol, monitoring of conditions, and actions reasonably necessary to preserve order, protect persons and property, and ensure safe and lawful use of such lands and facilities;ACTIVE
21
6 H.C.S. § 1302(b)⤷Investigate violations of, and enforce, the Park Code and any other applicable County laws, rules, or regulations relating to parks, recreation, conservation, wildlife, hunting, fishing, off-road or recreational vehicle use, and the use and protection of natural resources, including by issuing citations, notices of violation, and orders to comply, and by directing persons to cease prohibited conduct or to leave County lands and facilities administered by HCPR when necessary to enforce such laws or protect public safety;ACTIVE
22
6 H.C.S. § 1302(c)⤷Investigate and enforce County laws and regulations relating to hunting, fishing, wildlife, conservation, and the protection of natural resources throughout Harrison County, provided that such authority shall not include the power to make arrests;ACTIVE
23
6 H.C.S. § 1302(d)⤷Temporarily detain a person when the Ranger has probable cause to believe that the person has committed an offense in the Ranger’s presence, or presents an immediate and articulable threat to life, serious bodily harm, or imminent and substantial harm to public safety, provided that any such detention is limited to the period reasonably necessary to stabilize the situation and transfer custody to the Sheriff or other lawful authority;ACTIVE
24
6 H.C.S. § 1302(e)⤷Respond to emergencies or public safety incidents outside County lands and facilities administered by HCPR when requested or directed by the Sheriff’s Office or another lawful public safety agency, or when the Ranger directly observes conduct presenting an immediate and articulable threat to life or serious bodily harm, and take reasonable action consistent with 7 H.C.S. § 1007(d) to protect life and safety until relieved by sworn law enforcement or proper emergency personnel;ACTIVE
25
6 H.C.S. § 1302(f)⤷Carry and use such equipment as is authorized by the Department and reasonably necessary for the performance of assigned duties, operate Department-authorized vehicles, including emergency vehicles, and use reasonable and proportionate force when necessary to carry out lawful duties, prevent resistance, protect themselves or others, or effectuate a lawful detention under this Act, consistent with Department policy and applicable law;ACTIVE
26
6 H.C.S. § 1302(g)⤷Coordinate with the Sheriff’s Office and comply with lawful requests of the District Attorney made in furtherance of prosecution, consistent with the Charter and County law.ACTIVE
