Oversize loads may, upon request of the operator of the commercial vehicle carrying the oversize load, be escorted by law enforcement officers authorized in 6 H.C.S. § 2402(b)(i) to conduct CVE operations to ensure safe transportation of contents and the safety of other motorists.​
190
9 H.C.S. § 2301(a)⤷Law enforcement officers providing escorts for oversize loads shall travel ahead of the oversize load and clear intersections as necessary for the commercial vehicle to safely reach its destination.​

192
9 H.C.S. § 2301(c)⤷Escorts may not be used to break traffic laws except where absolutely necessary for an oversize load to reach its destination.​
