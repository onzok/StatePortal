Subchapter I: Definitions
71
11 H.C.S. § 3101“Destructive Device” means any conventional, improvised, or homemade device capable of exploding, detonating, lighting aflame, or producing destructive force through the use of explosive or incendiary materials.ACTIVE
72
11 H.C.S. § 3101(a)⤷Motor vehicles are not considered destructive devices under this section.ACTIVE
73
11 H.C.S. § 3101(b)⤷Firearms or firearm ammunition are not considered destructive devices under this section.ACTIVE
74
11 H.C.S. § 3101(c)⤷Empty propane tanks are not considered destructive devices under this section.ACTIVE
75
11 H.C.S. § 3102“Illegal Communication Device” means any digital, radio, or electronic communication device that has been altered from its factory configuration to transmit, receive, or boost signals intended to arm, trigger, or detonate a destructive device.ACTIVE
76
11 H.C.S. § 3103“Ingredient of a Destructive Device” means any item, thing, or device which is a precursor ingredient to creating a destructive device.ACTIVE
77
11 H.C.S. § 3103(a)⤷Empty propane tanks are considered ingredients of a destructive device under this section.ACTIVE
78
11 H.C.S. § 3103(b)⤷Tape is not considered an ingredient of a destructive device under this section unless found with multiple other ingredients.ACTIVE

83
11 H.C.S. § 3301Notwithstanding any other provision of the Arson and Explosives Control Ordinance, an amnesty period of seven (7) days shall commence on the effective date of the Arson and Explosives Control Ordinance. During this period, any person in possession of an item that becomes prohibited under the Arson and Explosives Control Ordinance may voluntarily surrender such item to the Harrison County Militia or Harrison County Sheriff's Office without being subject to criminal prosecution or civil penalties for possession of the item, provided that:ACTIVE
84
11 H.C.S. § 3301(a)⤷The item is surrendered voluntarily before any law enforcement officer has initiated contact regarding its unlawful possession;ACTIVE
85
11 H.C.S. § 3301(b)⤷The individual is otherwise lawfully eligible to possess the item at the time of surrender, except for the prohibition established by the Arson and Explosives Control Ordinance;ACTIVE
86
11 H.C.S. § 3301(c)⤷The surrender is conducted in accordance with procedures established by the aforementioned agencies.ACTIVE
87
11 H.C.S. § 3302Upon expiration of the amnesty period, possession of any item prohibited by the Arson and Explosives Control Ordinance shall be subject to the penalties and enforcement provisions set forth therein.ACTIVE
